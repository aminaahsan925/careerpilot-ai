## AI-label warning

5 of the best 8 are labelled "AI modified" on Pinterest (pins 1, 2, 4, 7, 8).
Only pins 3 (READER), 5 (Plopp) and 6 (Loop) showed no AI label. Treat the
AI-modified pins as mood / composition references only — rebuild with real
photography, original illustration and a real mascot — and prefer the three
non-labelled pins as the closest to real designer work.

## 3 distinct visual directions

1. **Poster Brutalism: type is the hero.** Giant condensed type, black/white/
   red/acid yellow, numbered rows instead of cards.
2. **Editorial Magazine.** CareerPilot as a magazine: masthead, cover lines,
   magazine sections for jobs and roadmaps.
3. **Character-Led / Living World.** Playful, illustration-led, interactive —
   a mascot with a face, a living scene, a dashboard organised as places.

## Recommendation from the evidence

Test Direction 1 (Poster Brutalism) for the landing page — it is the furthest
from the rejected dashboard — with Direction 3's Plopp/Loop mascot as the AI
Recruiter's face inside it, and Direction 2's magazine sections for jobs and
roadmaps. Avoid reusing the rejected dark violet glow, light indigo cards, or
KPI strips in any direction.
