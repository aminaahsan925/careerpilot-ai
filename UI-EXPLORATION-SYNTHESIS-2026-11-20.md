Synthesised from 4 sources, 28 references studied 20 Nov 2026:

- Figma Community: UI-EXPLORATION-FIGMA-2026-11-20.md (10 files, 6 best)

All four researchers independently recommended a hybrid (different direction
per screen). The three directions below are the pure options; the hybrid is
offered as a fourth pick.

## Direction 1 — Soft Indigo AI SaaS ("the friendly dashboard")

Feel: light, friendly, rounded. Lavender-grey ground, white cards, one vivid
indigo/violet accent, big readiness and match numbers. AI is a coach that gives
specific, actionable guidance, not a black box.

Palette: lavender-grey #F1F1F9 ground, pale lavender #C6BBF4, charcoal #2F3439,
vivid blue-violet #1E0CD5 / indigo #3F31CD; stat cards tinted lavender, pale
blue, mint, peach.
Type: bold geometric/grotesque sans, very large numerals, small uppercase
micro-labels.

What it changes in CareerPilot:
- Dashboard: 4 KPI cards + recommended jobs + career journey timeline (PADOS).
- Resume/profile: big score donut ("87% complete, almost there!") + skill-gap
  insight cards.
- Recruiter: readiness score out of 100 + simulation cards with coach names +
  AI coach chat bubble (ElevateAI).
- Market mirror: KPI + bar + donut + jobs table (CareerHub skeleton).
- Landing: prompt-first hero, the page itself is the demo — paste a resume or
  type a goal first (Larka AI, Nova AI).

## Direction 2 — Dark Analytics / Cinematic AI ("the serious one")

Feel: near-black, glowing charts, one electric accent, game mechanics. The AI
has a presence, almost a character. Progress feels like a quest with stakes,
not a checklist.

Palette: near-black #040405 / aubergine-black #1C0B34, one electric accent
(lime green, or vivid violet #6D2D93 / #642BB4), white numerals, magenta/pink
gradients for the character moments.
Type: light, spacious grotesque sans with very large white numbers; grotesk
headlines with an italic serif second line for the human counterpoint.

What it changes in CareerPilot:
- Market mirror: dark analytics grid — KPI strip, bar chart with one
  highlighted bar, donut, weekday/hour heatmap, lime AI-insight card (Pulse).
- Roadmap: streaks, points, study-flow rings, week challenge, skill % rings —
  motivation without hiding honest stats (Mindzy).
- Recruiter: the AI as a character; screening as a quest — three worlds
  (resume, skills, job market), shield = hireability, missions = fixes (Cyera,
  Imogen).
- Landing: dark hero + a live "your hireability" calculator with sliders
  (skills, experience, target roles → match %, salary uplift) (Imogen, 11Sight).

dark dashboard risks tiring the eyes on long roadmap sessions.

## Direction 3 — Warm Editorial, Colour-Led ("the human one")

Feel: paper ground, editorial type, colour does the wayfinding. Each product
area owns a hue, so the student always knows where they are. Timelines instead
of tables. Resume gaps shown as coded findings with a severity and a fix, like
an inspector's report. Warm, human, distinctive. The closest evolution of
CareerPilot's current ink/terracotta style.

Palette: paper/cream ground, near-black ink, terracotta kept OR one strong
accent (signal red #DA2520, orange-red #F15534, or emerald green); craft hues
per area (lavender, teal, lime, amber, pink, yellow, sky blue).
Type: bold editorial grotesque, condensed all-caps headlines + monospace labels
and coordinates; oversized poster type on the landing page.

What it changes in CareerPilot:
- Roadmap: a vertical timeline with colour-coded stages and fact chips, not a
  table (ACANAV, CambriLearn).
- Resume analysis: each gap as a coded "violation" card — code, severity
  (CRITICAL / HIGH), status, fix — with a drag-to-reveal before/after resume
  (Heron).
- Jobs: posted-notice cards with a perforated edge; the tear-off tab IS the
  apply control; each role's hue follows it everywhere (Open Call).
- Recruiter: kanban pipeline Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer, student
  as a candidate card with a match score (ATS UI Kit).
- Landing: poster headline + highlighter + colour blocking (NextHire, Jobelia);
  warm human AI with a real photo and a chat mockup (Zoe).
- Student touch: encouraging copy + a friendly avatar on greetings and empty
  states (Pastel Clay).

scale across dense data views, and the posted-notice pattern is unusual — it
will stand out, which is the point, but it must stay usable.

## The hybrid (what all four researchers recommended)

Direction 1 for the main dashboard and resume score (friendly, legible),
Direction 2 for the market mirror and the recruiter hero (dark, alive),
Direction 3 for the roadmap, the resume findings and the landing voice (warm,
editorial, colour-led). One product, three moods, each where it fits best.
