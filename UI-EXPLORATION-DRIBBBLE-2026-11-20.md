# CareerPilot UI/UX exploration — Dribbble findings (2026-11-20)

Browser study, no sign-in. 6 searches, 10 shots opened, 8 best below.
Source task: browser-task:0de75859-62e5-53a1-af4b-bf412a910396 (ran 16:37-16:41 PKT).

## 3 visual directions seen on Dribbble

1. **Light, soft indigo/lavender AI SaaS** — most directly transferable to
   CareerPilot. Friendly, rounded, pastel-tinted dashboards and a prompt-first
   landing page. Large readiness/match numbers, one vivid indigo/violet accent,
   white cards, AI as a coach/insight. Best for: main dashboard, resume/profile
   score, AI recruiter.
2. **Dark, data-dense analytics / gamified motivation** — near-black surfaces,
   glowing charts, game mechanics (streaks, points, rings). Best for: job-market
   mirror, and making the learning roadmap motivating rather than a checklist.
3. **Warm editorial / playful bold** — closest evolution of CareerPilot's
   current ink/terracotta style. Editorial grotesque type, a strong red or
   green accent, timeline/path layouts, bold graphic shapes. Best for: learning
   roadmap/academic path, job search, marketing/landing pages.

  charcoal #2F3439, grey #ACB3B4 / #81809A, vivid blue-violet #1E0CD5 and

## Suggested synthesis (from the researcher)

Direction 1 as the product core (PADOS + ElevateAI for dashboard/readiness,
Sakrome for the recruiter), Direction 2 for the two places that need energy and
proof (Outcrowd for the market mirror, Tino/Mindzy for the roadmap's streaks
and rings), Direction 3 as the landing/roadmap voice (ACANAV's timeline,
Jobelia's bold job search, Zoe's warm human AI).
